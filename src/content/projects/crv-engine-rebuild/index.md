## The problem

A snapped timing belt on an interference engine means the pistons and valves
occupy the same space at the same time. On this 2001 Honda CR-V the result was
bent valves and a cylinder head that had to come off.

## What I did

A full upper-engine rebuild: complete teardown, repair of the cylinder head, and
reassembly — done over a single winter while carrying coursework and a part-time
job.

## Result

A running engine. Beyond the repair itself, this is the project that taught me
how much of mechanical work is sequencing and patience: timing components are
unforgiving about order, and there's no step you can skip because you're tired
of the job.
