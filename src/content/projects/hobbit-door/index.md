## The problem

A round door is a carpentry problem disguised as a novelty. It has to actually
hang, swing, and seal — and a circle gives you none of the right angles that
normal door framing quietly depends on.

The project also started smaller than it finished. I chose to scale it well past
the original requirement, to a full **8-foot functional door**.

## What I did

CAD modeling first, then three months of fabrication: woodworking and carpentry
to get from a model to a door that opens and closes.

## Result

A finished 8-foot working Hobbit door. The useful lesson was the gap between a
model that closes cleanly in CAD and a physical assembly built from material that
has grain, moisture, and its own opinions about staying flat.
