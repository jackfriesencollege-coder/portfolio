## The idea

To celebrate my girlfriend's birthday one year, I wanted to surprise her with something special. She absolutely loves rom-coms, and had never been to the drive-in, but always had wanted to. I thought I could get two birds with one stone and design my own drive-in movie theater, and surprise her with a drive-in Rom-Com movie night. 

## The Prototype

I started with a pipe-connecting toy kit to make the first design. It worked decenlty well, but it took up a lot of space and was hard to take apart quickly.

(Insert new image I just sent you of the pipe-based screen, with the cloth attached)

## Design constraints

Due to the failures of the first version, it became nescessary to add constraints for the second draft. The largest three constraints were:
1. Quick and easy to assemble and disassemble
2. small and compact
3. Cheaper than buying a screen online

## CAD

To adress these problems, I started by making a desing in Fusion 360. I made the body of the frame out of PVC, while the joints were made out of custom 3d prints to aid in the ease of set up and tear down.

After desinging and printing the parts, it was finally time to put it all together.

The whole thing breaks down into a bundle of pipe that fits in the car alongside everything else.

## Result

A working portable screen that sets up on flat ground and displays a movie clearly. Most importantly, it made for an amazing drive-in experience. 

![The view from inside the car: a movie playing on the screen through the windshield](./drivein.jpg)

## What I learned

This project taugth me the value of placing constraints on a system before designing. The prototype worked for what we needed it to, but in reality we never would have gone again unless I had redesigned it around these constraints

In the real world, this means listening to the client and emphatizing with their situation before putting pen to paper. A fundamental understanding of the problem you are trying to solve will lead to the greatest solutions.
