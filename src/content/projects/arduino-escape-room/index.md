## The problem

Build a working escape room in six weeks, as a four-person team, with the software
and the hardware both having to hold up in front of an audience that gets exactly
one try.

## What I did

I led the team through design and build: a winter-themed escape room running a
**background timer**, with **MATLAB talking to an Arduino** to drive the puzzle
logic, and a **custom 3D-printed controller** as the physical interface.

The harder part wasn't technical. Partway through, the team wasn't going to finish
with its current makeup, and I had to remove an underperforming member and
redistribute the work with the deadline unchanged. That was the actual leadership
challenge of the project, and it's the part I learned the most from.

## Result

A finished, working escape room delivered on schedule — and a much more concrete
sense of what it costs to make a personnel call rather than hope a problem
resolves itself.
