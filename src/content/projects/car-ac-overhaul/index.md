## The problem

An automotive A/C system that wasn't cooling. The symptom is simple; the cause
usually isn't, since a low charge is a consequence rather than a root cause —
refrigerant doesn't get consumed, it escapes.

## What I did

I traced the system down to a refrigerant leak, then overhauled it rather than
just topping it up: replaced the **compressor**, **expansion valve**, and
**condenser**, flushed the system to clear debris from the failed components,
and recharged it.

## Result

A working A/C system, and a hands-on understanding of the vapor-compression
refrigeration cycle that has since made the thermodynamics coursework a lot less
abstract — I'd already watched the pressure and temperature change across each
stage with a gauge set in my hand.
