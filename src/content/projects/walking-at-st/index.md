## The problem

A two-legged walker is a genuinely hard mechanism. It has to carry its own weight,
stay balanced through the gait, and do it with linkages and actuators that fit
inside the shape of the thing you're modeling — which, for an AT-ST, was not
designed by anyone worried about statics.

## What I did

I've been designing and iteratively refining a scale AT-ST in **Fusion 360** since
2023, with **Arduino-based control** driving the motion.

"Iteratively" is doing real work in that sentence. This project has been the
place I go to learn something and then find out where my model was wrong —
mechanism design, electronics, and the programming to tie them together.

## Result

Still ongoing, which is the point. It's the project where I get to be wrong
cheaply, and most of what I know about robotics and electronic systems started
with something on this model not working.
